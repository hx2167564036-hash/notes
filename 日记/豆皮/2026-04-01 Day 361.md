# 361 天


是美妙的星期三，对于这个学期的课程安排来说，星期三简直就是学习过程中，让我喘息的一天，虽然平时貌似也没有累到需要喘息的程度。最近都一直无法睡懒觉，早上又是八九点就醒了。早上上完课后，就没有课程安排了。中午又是跑去吃了自助烤肉，看来周三已经成为固定的放纵日，但是我觉得也不算太放纵吧，毕竟今天也只吃这一顿。

晚上有我雅思听力课的首课，所以下午的时候也是提早就来到了上课的地方，提前做好了上课的准备。我觉得听力还是值得我冲一冲高分的，毕竟本来听力的水平也还不错。在我的口语及写作还没有提升到一定水平的时候，我还是希望我的听力和阅读能够起到一个拉高平均分的作用。

Day 361, and it’s such a lovely Wednesday! For this semester’s class schedule, Wednesday’s totally a breather in my study grind—though let’s be real, I don’t even feel that worn out to need one most days anyway. I haven’t been able to sleep in lately; I just keep waking up at 8 or 9 in the morning. After my morning classes, there’s no more schoolwork for the day. I went for a barbecue buffet again at lunch, so Wednesday’s basically my regular cheat day now. But I don’t think it’s even that much of a cheat—after all, this is the only big meal I’m having today.

I’ve got the first class of my IELTS listening course tonight, so I headed to the classroom early this afternoon to get all set for it. I reckon listening’s definitely worth aiming for a high score in; my listening skills are pretty solid to begin with. Until my speaking and writing get up to scratch, I’m hoping my listening and reading can help boost my average score.

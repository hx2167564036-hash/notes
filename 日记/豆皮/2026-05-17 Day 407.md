# 407 天


今天干的事还真不少，虽然是周日，但是出于一些对于昨天什么事都没干的愧疚感，我今天醒的还是比较早的。自律的是一起床就做完了三篇听力和一篇阅读hhh，值得表扬。

今天下午去找w去爬了个山hh，说明两个人还是蛮有精力的。找了一个不是很高，不是很辛苦的山，是宝石山。虽然说看起来海拔不高，也没那么艰难，但是爬起来也确实不是很轻松。不知道为什么，可能人类的本能中就还有一些看到石头就想攀登上去的原始欲望hh，我们今天一直不断的在攀登大石头hh。然后也是第一次以这个视角看到了西湖。

到了吃饭的时间，两个人也异常的没有感觉到任何饥饿。于是又走来走去，一直到走不动为止。最终在一家浇头面馆坐下，完成了一餐简简单单的晚饭。

Wow, 407 days have flown by!

Though it’s Sunday, I woke up pretty early—felt kinda guilty about doing nothing yesterday, so I wanted to make today count. And guess what? I was super disciplined: as soon as I got out of bed, I finished three listening exercises and a reading passage. Go me! 😂

This afternoon, I met up with W to climb a mountain—turns out we still have tons of energy! We picked a not-too-high, not-too-tough one called Baoshi Mountain. Even though it looked low-key, climbing wasn’t exactly a breeze. Not sure why, but maybe there’s some primal urge in humans to climb rocks? We kept scaling big stones the whole time. And hey, it was my first time seeing West Lake from this angle—totally worth it!

By dinner time, neither of us felt hungry at all. We just wandered around until we couldn’t walk anymore, then finally stopped at a noodle shop with lots of toppings. Simple dinner, but perfect after all that hiking.

All in all, a pretty packed (but fun!) day. 😊

# 2026-06-13 22:13:09


Day 434 Saturday

It was an incredibly draining day.There was a CET Band 6 English test at 3 pm, which meant that I couldn't have a proper relaxing weekend .

I woke up at around 10:30 a.m. I had slept for quite a long time, but I still felt groggy .Afterwards, I quickly worked through two reading practices, took several screenshots of my ealier listening practice results ,and sent them to my teacher.

The CET test lasted about two and a half hours ,and I finished it with a great relief.To sum up briefly,The listening section was quite tricky for me, because it's totally different from the IELTS listening.You listen to the audio first, and the questions only appear at the end of the recordings . This format really ==threw me off（打乱节奏）==.I'm not used to that style of question,which is why I think it's more difficult than IELTS listening.

But for the reading, translation and writing, I felt more fluent and confident .I owe this to my growing vocabulary.It made it easier for me to understand the questions and passages. Meanwhile,the phrases and expressions I've built up have made writing and translation feel simpler too.

After the test, I was hit with such a wave of exhaustion that I didn't feel like doing anything at all. So I left my dorm and went for a slow,aimless walk with no destinations in mind.
